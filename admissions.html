Admission Process

  1. Contact the school for admission information.
  2. Ask about available classes and spaces.
  3. Obtain the admission application form.
  4. Complete and submit the required information.
  5. Follow the school's instructions for the next stage of admission.

Admission Requirements

Parents and guardians will be informed about the documents and requirements needed during the admission process.

  • Completed admission application form
  • Child's relevant school records, where applicable
  • Passport photographs
  • Other documents requested by the school
