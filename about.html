About Comely Light Academy

Comely Light Academy is a Christian educational institution committed to raising godly, knowledgeable and responsible children through faith, wisdom and excellence.

Our Motto

“Where God's Light Leads to Excellence.”

Our Vision

To be a leading Christian educational institution, raising godly, knowledgeable, and responsible leaders who will positively impact their generation through faith, wisdom, and excellence.

Our Mission

To provide quality education in a Christ-centered environment that nurtures academic excellence, godly character, creativity, discipline, and leadership skills, enabling every child to fulfill God's purpose for their life.

Our Core Values

  • Godliness – Upholding Christian faith and biblical principles.
  • Excellence – Striving for the highest standards in learning and conduct.
  • Integrity – Promoting honesty, accountability, and moral uprightness.
  • Discipline – Encouraging self-control, diligence, and responsibility.
  • Love – Demonstrating compassion, respect, and care for others.
  • Service – Developing a heart for serving God, humanity, and the community.
  • Leadership – Preparing students to be positive influencers and role models.

School Scripture

"Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven."

— Matthew 5:16 (KJV)

School Creed

"At Comely Light Academy, we walk in God's light, pursue excellence, speak the truth, show love, respect others, and strive to be the best God has created us to be. With faith, knowledge, and good character, we will shine brightly and make a positive difference in our world."
